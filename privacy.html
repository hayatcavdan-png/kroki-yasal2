YouTube API Services

KROKİ Studio Uploader uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. YouTube's use of data is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

The tool is an internal application operated solely by the channel owner for the owner's own YouTube channels. No other person can sign in.

What data is accessed

  • Channel identity (ID, title) to verify the correct channel before uploading.
  • Upload results (video ID, status, privacy) of videos uploaded by the tool.
  • Performance metrics of the owner's own channel (views, watch time, average view percentage, subscribers gained) and comments on the owner's videos (read only).

How data is used and shared

Data is used only to publish the owner's videos and plan future content. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Storage and retention

Data is stored in a private, access-controlled repository. YouTube API data is deleted or refreshed within 30 days. OAuth tokens are stored encrypted.

Deleting your data / revoking access

You can revoke the tool's access at any time at Google security settings. Stored data is deleted within 30 days of revocation, or immediately on request to srknlc73@gmail.com.

Cookies

The tool does not use cookies or tracking on viewers.
